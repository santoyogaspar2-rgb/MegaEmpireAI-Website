MegaEmpireAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. About MegaEmpireAI

MegaEmpireAI is an AI-assisted content creation and management application under development. It is designed to help users prepare, organize, review, and publish original digital content through authorized accounts.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, MegaEmpireAI may process account identifiers, profile information, user-provided content, and authorization tokens granted through supported social media platforms.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide requested application features, manage content, support authorized publishing, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Social Media Integrations

Social media access requires user authorization. MegaEmpireAI will request only permissions necessary for supported features and will not publish content without the user's authorization.

5. Data Sharing

Information may be transmitted to authorized platform APIs and necessary service providers to perform requested functions. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as necessary for legitimate application purposes. Users may request access to or deletion of their information by contacting us. Platform permissions may also be revoked through the relevant platform settings.

8. Children's Privacy

MegaEmpireAI is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when application features or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page.
